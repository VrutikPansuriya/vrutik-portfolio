Project overview

The project & the goal.

Souls-like Combat Systems is an independent third-person action prototype focused on responsive melee encounters and readable enemy behaviour. I built it as a hands-on project to explore the interconnected systems that shape this style of combat.

The prototype brings together player combat abilities, target locking, dodge mechanics, adaptive health and damage behaviour, enemy perception, boss encounters, and interactive water gameplay.

My responsibilities: As the independent developer, I designed and implemented the gameplay systems, player abilities, health and damage logic, enemy AI behaviours, and interaction features showcased in the prototype.

Work breakdown

Systems I worked on.

01

Melee combat foundation

Developed the player combat loop and supporting gameplay logic for a third-person action experience, with a focus on responsive actions and clear combat feedback.

02

Target lock & dodge

Implemented target-lock functionality and dodge mechanics to support deliberate positioning, enemy tracking, and evasion during encounters.

03

Adaptive health & damage

Built health and damage behaviour intended to support combat situations and different sources of incoming damage.

04

Enemy perception & boss behaviour

Worked on enemy perception and combat behaviour, including a boss encounter that gives the player a more demanding test of the core mechanics.

05

Swimming & interactive water

Implemented swimming and interactive water mechanics to extend the prototype beyond ground-based combat and support environmental gameplay.

06

Playable Windows build

Prepared a Windows build so the prototype can be experienced outside the editor, alongside a gameplay video demonstrating the systems in action.

Engineering notes

Why I built this project

This personal project lets me experiment with gameplay feel and system architecture without the constraints of a production brief. It also acts as a practical demonstration of how I approach player mechanics, AI, combat feedback, and connected gameplay features in Unreal Engine.

The emphasis throughout was on building gameplay features that are understandable, testable, and dependable in a playable build.
